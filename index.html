The Maison

Half a century of obsession with the measure of time.

Founded in a small atelier in 1971, PETROCORE was built on a single conviction — that a watch should be an instrument first and an ornament second. Every caliber is assembled, regulated and finished by hand, then tested for fifteen days before it earns our name on the dial.

The craft behind the dial

Four disciplines, one timepiece. Each pillar is held to a standard that takes years to master and seconds to feel.

01

In-house movement

The PC-71 automatic caliber — 27 jewels, 72-hour reserve, free-sprung balance — designed, machined and regulated under our own roof.

→
02

Hand finishing

Anglage, Côtes de Genève and perlage applied by hand. Over 200 hours of finishing on surfaces you will rarely see — but always sense.

→
03

Materials

316L and grade-5 titanium cases, box-sapphire crystal, and luminescent indices engineered to glow through the darkest hours.

→
04

Precision testing

Fifteen days on the timing machine across six positions. Chronometer-grade accuracy, certified before it ever reaches your wrist.

→
05

Architecture

An exhibition caseback frames a skeletonised rotor in motion — the engine of the watch, on permanent display.

→
06

Guarantee

A five-year international warranty and a promise of service for the lifetime of the timepiece. Built to be inherited.

About HORO PRIVÉ

A new standard inprivate watch advisory.

The right watch. The right price. The right moment.

HORO PRIVÉ was created around a simple belief: a collector should not be limited to the watches held by one shop.

We begin with the client — not the inventory.

Through independent guidance, international sourcing, careful evaluation, discreet negotiation and intelligent portfolio management, we help clients make better decisions throughout the ownership journey.

Whether the objective is a first significant watch, a rare reference, a collection upgrade, or the long-term management of multiple timepieces, HORO PRIVÉ acts as the collector's private watch office.

The name

Horology, heldin private.

“Horo” is drawn from horology — the art and science of measuring time, and of the watchmaking we have devoted ourselves to understanding.

“Privé” is French for private — private access, exclusivity, and discreet, personal service reserved for those we represent.

Together: a private house of horology. Not a shop you walk into, but an office that works for you — quietly, knowledgeably, and only in your interest.

Important information

Valuation disclaimer

Market valuations shown anywhere on this platform are estimates for guidance only. They are not offers, appraisals or guarantees. Historical price performance does not guarantee future appreciation, and watches should not be regarded as guaranteed investments. We do not guarantee liquidity or resale value.

Authentication disclaimer

Authentication based on images is preliminary. Final verification may require physical inspection by a qualified professional. We coordinate verification and due diligence; external sellers, inspectors and service providers may be independent parties. We never imply guaranteed authenticity from photographs alone.

Sourcing & availability

HORO PRIVÉ is an advisory and sourcing service. Availability, condition and pricing are confirmed during review and can change. Every proposed opportunity is subject to verification, seller checks and commercial assessment.

Taxes & duties

Customs, duties and taxes depend on the destination and applicable law and are estimates only — not final tax advice. Clients remain responsible for reviewing final transaction documents.

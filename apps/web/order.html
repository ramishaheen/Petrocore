Good to know

Everything you might want to ask before you commission.

Do I pay when I place the order?
No. Your commission is a reservation. A client advisor confirms availability, specification and pricing with you personally before any payment is arranged — securely and at your pace.
How long does a commission take?
Signature references typically ship within 3–6 weeks. Fully bespoke pieces and grand complications are hand-built to order and usually take 8–16 weeks. Your advisor will confirm an exact timeline.
Is my information secure?
Always. Your details are transmitted over an encrypted connection, used only to fulfil your commission, and never shared or sold. You can request deletion at any time.
What does the warranty cover?
Every PETROCORE carries a five-year international warranty against manufacturing defects, plus lifetime access to servicing by our own watchmakers.
Can I return or exchange?
Yes — any unworn reference may be returned within 14 days for a full refund or exchange. Engraved bespoke pieces are assessed individually by your advisor.

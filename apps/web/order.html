Good to know

How representation works, before you begin.

Is there any cost or obligation?
The initial consultation is complimentary and carries no obligation. Where a sourcing or management engagement follows, fees are agreed transparently in advance.
Do you hold the watches you show?
Sometimes, but our role is to represent you across the whole market — authorized dealers, auction houses and private collectors — rather than to sell from a fixed inventory.
How do you verify a watch?
We coordinate seller checks, document and reference-consistency review, condition assessment and, where appropriate, independent physical inspection and escrow — before any funds move. Authentication based on images alone is preliminary.
Are valuations guaranteed?
No. Market valuations are estimates and historical performance does not guarantee future appreciation. We never represent a watch as a guaranteed investment.
Is my information private?
Yes. Your details are used only to assist you, transmitted over an encrypted connection, and never shared or sold. You can request deletion at any time.
